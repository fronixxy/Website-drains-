    Save money

    City rebates for flood protection

    Toronto and several GTA municipalities offer subsidies to homeowners who install flood-protection devices. Programs typically cover part of the cost of:

    • Mainline backwater valve
    • Sump pump
    • Pipe severance and capping of the foundation drain
    • Downspout disconnection (some cities)

    Amounts, eligibility and paperwork differ by city and change from year to year. Most programs require a permit and an inspection before you apply — we prepare the invoices and documents you need.

    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    Interior or exterior waterproofing — which one do I need?

    Exterior waterproofing stops water before it reaches the wall and is the most complete solution, but it costs more and needs room to dig. Interior waterproofing collects water at the footing and sends it to a sump pump — it costs less and works where you can't excavate outside. After an inspection we recommend the option that fits your house and budget.

    Why is my basement leaking?

    The most common causes in the GTA are cracks in the foundation, clogged or collapsed clay weeping tile, poor grading, downspouts discharging next to the wall and hydrostatic pressure from high groundwater. Sewer backup during storms is a separate problem solved with a backwater valve.

    Do I need a permit?

    In Toronto and most GTA cities a plumbing permit is required for backwater valves, sump pumps, pipe severance and sewer work. We apply for permits and book city inspections for you; permit fees are included in our price ranges.

    Can you work in winter?

    Yes. Interior waterproofing, drain work and valve installations run all year. Exterior excavation is possible in winter, but frozen ground can add to the cost.

    What does the warranty cover?

    Waterproofing comes with a written, transferable warranty of up to 25 years against water coming through the treated area. Pumps and valves carry the manufacturer's warranty plus our workmanship warranty.

    Are the prices on this site final?

    No. They are typical ranges to help you plan a budget. Your exact price is confirmed in a written quote after a free on-site inspection.
